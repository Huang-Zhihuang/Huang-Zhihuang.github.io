Zhihuang Huang is a PhD student in Animal–Computer Interaction (ACI) in the School of Computing Science at the University of Glasgow, supervised by Dr Ilyena Hirskyj-Douglas and Professor Mohamed Khamis. His research focuses on companion animals, particularly cats in domestic and veterinary contexts, and examines how interactive technologies can respect animal agency, support mutual well-being, and strengthen human–cat relationships during periods of separation and stress.

During his master’s studies, Zhihuang conducted research under the supervision of Professor Gang Ren and Professor Gang Wang, focusing on multimodal feedback for vulnerable road users, human–robot interaction and wearable systems.
